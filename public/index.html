HOW TO PLAY

  • Every turn ALL players choose at the same time. When everyone has chosen (or the clock runs out) all moves happen at once.
  • MOVE 1 tile. Two dicks going for the same tile bonk heads and both stay put.
  • SHOOT YOUR LOAD at any tile in range. Whoever is standing there after the moves gets splatted. Predict where they'll go!
  • A hit knocks off one of the victim's BALLS. It rolls nearby — walk over it to collect it.
  • Range = 1 + your balls. More balls, longer shots.
  • Lose both balls and you're out. Last dick standing wins.
  • JUNK: fridges, tire stacks & trash heaps block moving AND shooting (take cover!). Toilets, tires & bags only block moving — loads arc over them.
  • After turn 30: SUDDEN DEATH — the dump crumbles (flashing tiles are about to fall) and knocked-off balls fly away for good.

BALL POWERS

Balls with a glowing ring carry a power. Pick one up: +1 ball AND the power. Hover any tile to see what's on it.

  • BUKKAKE BOMB — your next load splashes the target and all 6 tiles around it (careful up close).
  • GONORRHEA MONKEY — next load gives the clap: victim loses a ball in 3 turns and infects anyone standing next to them.
  • BLUE BALLS — next load freezes the victim solid for a turn. Sitting duck.
  • DOUBLE PENETRATION — next load flies straight through junk and hits everyone in the line.
  • PRINCE ALBERT — a ring through the tip. Blocks one hit (stacks to 2). Useless against the clap.
  • VIAGRA — +3 range for 3 turns.
  • LUBE — dash 2 tiles per move for 3 turns.

CONTROLS

LEFT CLICK = act in current mode · RIGHT CLICK = shoot
Q / 1 = MOVE mode · E / 2 = SHOOT mode · SPACE = toggle · S = stay · M = music
Move the mouse toward the screen edge to look around. Minimap top-right.
