HOW TO PLAY

  • Every turn ALL players choose at the same time. When everyone has chosen (or the clock runs out) all moves happen at once.
  • MOVE 1 tile. Two dicks going for the same tile bonk heads and both stay put.
  • SHOOT YOUR LOAD at any tile in range. Whoever is standing there after the moves gets splatted. Predict where they'll go!
  • A hit knocks off one of the victim's BALLS. It rolls nearby — walk over it to collect it.
  • Range = 1 + your balls. More balls, longer shots.
  • Lose both balls and you're out. Last dick standing wins.
  • After turn 30: SUDDEN DEATH — the arena edge crumbles (flashing tiles are about to fall) and knocked-off balls fly away for good.

CONTROLS

LEFT CLICK = act in current mode · RIGHT CLICK = shoot
Q / 1 = MOVE mode · E / 2 = SHOOT mode · SPACE = toggle · S = stay
