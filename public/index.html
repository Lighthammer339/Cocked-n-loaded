HOW TO PLAY

  • TURN BASED: everyone plans at the same time. Pick a tile, change your mind as often as you like, then hit END TURN. When every dick is ready (or the clock runs out) all orders happen at once.
  • MOVE (usually 1 tile). Two dicks going for the same tile bonk heads and both stay put.
  • SHOOT YOUR LOAD at a tile in range. Whoever stands there after the moves gets splatted. Predict where they'll go!
  • A hit knocks off one BALL. It rolls nearby — walk over it to grab it. Extra balls get stapled to your sack. Range = 1 + your balls (+ class bonus).
  • SACK LIMIT: nobody can hold more than half of the balls the game started with.
  • Lose all your balls and you're out. Last dick standing wins.
  • JUNK: fridges, tire stacks & trash heaps block moving AND shooting. Toilets, tires & bags only block moving.
  • HOLES: cracked ground opens into a hole at the end of the turn. Standing on it = fall in, lose a ball, climb out nearby.
  • After turn 30: SUDDEN DEATH — the dump crumbles and knocked-off balls fly away for good.

THE DICKS

  • TURTLENECK — uncut ninja. STAY PUT = shell up and block every hit that turn.
  • BIG JOHNSON — starts with 3 huge balls, -1 range.
  • LONG DONG — sniper: +2 range but can't shoot adjacent tiles.
  • QUICKIE — moves 2 tiles per turn, -1 range.
  • DR. CLAP — immune to the clap; every 3rd load infects.
  • PIERCED PUNK — starts with a Prince Albert; rings stack to 3.

BALL POWERS & CURSES

Glowing balls carry a power; purple flickering ones are CURSED. Either way you get +1 ball. Hover anything in game for full stats.

  • BUKKAKE BOMB — next load splashes the target + all 6 tiles around it.
  • GONORRHEA MONKEY — next load gives the clap: -1 ball in 3 turns, contagious.
  • BLUE BALLS — next load freezes the victim for a turn.
  • DOUBLE PENETRATION — next load pierces junk and hits everyone in the line.
  • PRINCE ALBERT — ring through the tip, blocks one hit.
  • VIAGRA — +3 range for 3 turns.   LUBE — +1 move for 3 turns.
  • CURSES: WHISKEY DICK (can't shoot, 2 turns) · SHRINKAGE (range 1, 3 turns) · CRABS (moves go random, 2 turns).
